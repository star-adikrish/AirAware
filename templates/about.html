THE AQI PROJECT

Hi, I'm Krishna.

I made AirAware as a project to learn web development and to make it easier to check AQI for people who have asthma. I have noticed that many people in the northern side of India face different kinds of health issues out of which many are from air pollution so to bring this to their awareness i have built this website. I'm still learning, so it's a work in progress. Hope you find it useful!

WHY I MADE IT

Air quality, all in one place.

You can search for a city and see its AQI, particles, gases, and PM2.5 levels. I wanted the main numbers to be easy to find without jumping between different pages.One major reason also was that i noticed many people who have asthma or other respiratory issues struggle to find accurate aqi, but i know it was not possible to get accurate aqi from local sensors, so i made this project to help them get accurate aqi.
