A SMALL PROJECT WITH A CLEAR PURPOSE

Useful information, without the noise.

AirAware brings a city's AQI, particle levels, and common pollutants together in one place. Search for a city, see what the latest estimates say, and follow the PM2.5 trend through the day.

The goal is simple: make the numbers easier to read while being clear about what they are—API estimates, not readings from a local air monitor.
