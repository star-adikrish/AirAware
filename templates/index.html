INTERPRETATION NOTES i
  • AQI: US EPA-style index from 0 to 500; higher values indicate greater health concern.
  • Particles: PM2.5 and PM10 are reported as mass concentration in µg/m³.
  • Gases: CO, NO₂, SO₂, and ozone readings are reported in µg/m³ by the source API.
  • Scope: These are modelled hourly estimates, not a substitute for a certified local monitor.
